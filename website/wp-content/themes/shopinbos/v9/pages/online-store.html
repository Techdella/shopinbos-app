Talk to us
Do I need a laptop?

No. You can build and run your store entirely from your phone.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Start on a free yourshop.mybos.store address and connect your own domain on a paid plan.

Is my store part of a marketplace?

No. Your store is yours. Customers buy from you, on your link, under your name.

How do customers pay?

By card, bank transfer or pay on delivery. Money is paid out to your bank through Paystack.

Can I sell on WhatsApp and Instagram too?

Yes. Share products and payment links in any chat, and manage every DM from one inbox.

Your shop can be live tonight.

Tell Rex what you sell, add a few photos and share your link before bedtime. Free to start.
