01 · About

What Shopinbos is

Shopinbos is an AI commerce platform for African businesses. Thousands of sellers build online stores with our AI assistant Chip, and they need reliable products to sell. As a Shopinbos supplier, you list your stock once and sellers across Nigeria order it, either wholesale or dropship.

New buyers

Your products appear in the Shopinbos supplier marketplace, where sellers search for stock every day.

Dropship

Sellers list your products in their stores. When a customer buys, you ship it straight to the customer.

Wholesale

Sellers buy in bulk at your wholesale prices, or ask you for a quote.

Shopinbos is the software in the middle. We bring you buyers, handle checkout, arrange delivery pickups from your door, and split payments so your share goes straight to your bank.

Key datesSupplier registration is open from 22 October 2026. Shopinbos launches to sellers on 1 November 2026. Sign up early so your products are live on day one. Shopinbos currently operates in Nigeria, and prices are in Naira (₦).
02 · The big picture

How supplying works

  1. Sign up and tell us about your businessWhat you make or sell, where you're based and your product categories.
  2. Get verifiedUpload your CAC and product images. Our team checks them, usually within 1–2 business days.
  3. List your productsWholesale price, suggested retail price, minimum order, stock, and whether you offer dropship and wholesale.
  4. Sellers find youSellers browse the marketplace, add your products to their stores, place wholesale orders or ask for quotes.
  5. You accept and pack ordersAccept within the time limit, pack under the shop's label, and a rider collects it at a time you choose.

06 · Dropship

Dropship orders

When a customer buys your product from a seller's store, you get a dropship order (DS-XXXXXX). You're alerted on WhatsApp (or SMS), by email and by push notification.

Accept within 4 hours

You have 4 hours to accept a dropship order (night hours count). We remind you 60 minutes before the deadline. If you miss it, the order expires, the stock is released, it counts as a missed order, and it may be passed to another supplier with the same product.

The life of a dropship order

Pending→Accepted→Shipped→Delivered

Orders can also be Rejected, Cancelled or Expired. If you have to decline, give a reason; the shop sees it.

Packing rules

ImportantPack each order in its own packaging and stick on the shop's delivery label. Don't add flyers, your business card or your phone number. The customer belongs to the shop. Never contact a shop's customers to sell to them directly.

Shipping it

  • Rider pickup: pick a pickup time and a delivery partner collects from you (see section 08). You don't pay for delivery.
  • Deliver it yourself: you get a 4-digit delivery code from the customer, and you keep the delivery fee in full.
  • When shipping another way, add the tracking code.

Missed orders

Missed orders lower your trust score (−2 each) and show up to sellers as a risk. Keep your stock numbers up to date, and set stock to 0 or hide a product when you run out.

07 · Wholesale

Wholesale orders & quotes

Wholesale orders

Sellers can buy your products in bulk (PO-XXXXXX). The quantity must be at least your MOQ, and the unit price comes from your matching price tier (or your base wholesale price). Stock is reserved when the order is placed.

Pending→Confirmed→Processing→Shipped→Delivered
  • You have 24 hours to accept a wholesale order.
  • After you accept, the seller can pay by card in the app (100% goes to you) or pay you directly against an invoice.
  • A seller can cancel while the order is still pending or confirmed.

Quote requests

Sellers who want a custom price send a quote request with the quantity (at least your MOQ), their location and notes. Find them under Quote requests.

To reply

Enter a unit price, a message, and optionally a "valid until" date. The seller can accept it and order.

Not a fit?

Pass on it. The seller gets a polite note: "This request is not a fit for us right now."

Quote statuses: Waiting on youQuote sentAcceptedPassedExpired

TipReply quickly. Your response time shows on your profile and counts toward your trust score.
08 · Delivery

Delivery & rider pickups

You don't need your own riders. Shopinbos works with verified delivery partners who collect parcels from your door.

Booking a pickup

When an order is packed, choose when a rider should collect it. Pickups run 8am to 7pm (Lagos time) with at least 60 minutes' notice:

Morning

9–12

Afternoon

12–4

Evening

4–7

You can also pick a custom time up to 7 days ahead.

Who collects your parcel

  1. The delivery partner the shop chose at checkout, or
  2. One of your own connected delivery partners, or
  3. The Shopinbos default partner, or
  4. The most reliable verified partner covering your area.

If none is available, our team finds one for you.

Connecting your own delivery partners

Under Delivery you can browse verified delivery partners, compare their rates and connect with the ones you trust. Dropship parcels then go with your partners first. Free plan: 2 partners · Plus: 10 · Pro: unlimited. Plus and Pro can use private rate cards.

Delivering yourself

Prefer to deliver yourself? Mark the order as self-delivered. The customer gives you a 4-digit delivery code, and the delivery fee is all yours. Shopinbos takes no commission on delivery fees.

09 · Money in

Getting paid

Shopinbos never holds your money. When a customer or seller pays by card, Paystack splits the payment at checkout and your share goes straight to your bank account, usually the next working day.

A dropship sale, step by step

Customer pays the shop's price→Paystack splits it→Your wholesale price, less commission → your bank

The seller keeps their markup. Your commission and your share of Paystack's fee come out of your share.

Worked example (Free plan)

ItemAmount
Your wholesale price for a handbag₦20,000
Shopinbos commission (6%)−₦1,200
Your share before Paystack's fee₦18,800

Paystack's card fee is 1.5% + ₦100 (the ₦100 is waived on payments under ₦2,500, and the fee is capped at ₦2,000). The seller can't price your product below your cost plus fees, so you're always covered.

Set up your Shopinbos Wallet (one time)

  1. Add your bank accountChoose your bank and enter your 10-digit account number. We look up the name; tap "Yes, pay me here".
  2. Confirm your identityYour NIN or BVN (11 digits), your name and date of birth. You must be 18 or older. We keep only the last 4 digits.

Accounts that aren't in your name or your business's name are reviewed by our team, usually within a day.

No wallet yet?Until your wallet is ready, sellers pay you directly and you mark orders as paid under Earnings. No commission is taken on those orders, but you lose the protection and convenience of automatic payouts.

Pay on delivery

For pay-on-delivery orders, the rider collects cash from the customer and the shop pays you directly.

11 · Partnering

How sellers find & partner with you

Sellers search the Shopinbos supplier marketplace by product, category and location. Your public page lives at app.shopinbos.com/suppliers/your-name.

Dropship agreements

Before a seller can dropship your products, you agree to work together:

  1. The seller calls youThey check your stock, by visiting or by video call.
  2. The seller signs the dropship agreementAn e-signature that is legally binding under the Evidence Act 2011.
  3. You accept itUnder Buyers → Dropship agreements. Now they can list your products.

Under the agreement, your products must be genuine and carry NAFDAC/SON approval where required. Sellers resell your products as they are (it isn't private label).

Nearby sellersDropship works best locally: a seller can dropship your products when you're within 50 km of them or in the same city.

What sellers see about you

Sellers see your trust score and badge, rating, response time and a risk level (low, medium or high). To keep your risk low:

  • Get verified (the biggest factor)
  • Set up your wallet
  • Add a return policy
  • Don't miss or fail to send orders
  • Keep your rating above 3.5
  • Pay anything you owe on time

Ranking

In the marketplace, featured suppliers and products come first, then Pro suppliers, then Plus, then Free.

Refer & earn

Invite another supplier with your link (app.shopinbos.com/r/CODE). When they're verified with 5 approved products, you get 3 months of Supplier Plus or 500 AI credits, and they get 1 free month plus a featured week. Up to 10 rewards a year; extra rewards at 3, 5 and 10 referrals. Rewards are never paid in cash.

12 · Good standing

Returns, claims & rules

Your return policy

Add your own return policy (up to 1,500 characters) on your Company profile. If you don't, the Supplier Terms minimum applies.

What you're responsible for

  • Take back items that are wrong, damaged, faulty, not as described or short, and refund the shop.
  • Collect those returns, or pay for them to be sent back.
  • Change-of-mind returns are between the shop and the customer, unless your own policy offers them.

Claims & money owed

RuleDetail
Claim windowShops can claim within 7 days of delivery (wrong item, damaged, not as described, short delivery, not sent).
Paying a claimAccepted claims are due within 3 days.
If it's lateUp to 50% of each later order payment goes to the shop until it's covered.
7 days overdueYou're paused from new business: shops can't find your products and no new shop can list them. Existing orders continue. The pause lifts once you pay.

Disputes

Shopinbos decides disputes using what the app recorded plus evidence from both sides. Either side can ask for a review at legal@shopinbos.com.

Supplier rules at a glance

  • Hold a CAC registration and a business bank account in the same name.
  • Only list stock you can ship within 3 days, and ship within 3 days.
  • Pack under the shop's label, with none of your own marketing inside.
  • Never contact a shop's customers to sell to them directly.
  • Keep jobs and payments in Shopinbos. Payments arranged outside the app aren't protected.
QualityToo many returned, late or unsent orders can pause your listings.

14 · FAQ

Questions & support

Does it cost anything to join or list?

No. The Free (Verified) plan is ₦0 and listing is free. You only pay a commission on dropship sales.

How long does verification take?

Usually 1–2 business days after you submit your documents.

When do I get paid?

Card payments are split at checkout and usually reach your bank the next working day. Shopinbos never holds your money.

Do I need my own riders?

No. Book a pickup and a verified delivery partner collects from you. You can also connect your own delivery partners or deliver yourself.

What if I run out of stock?

Update your stock straight away (it changes instantly, no review needed) so you don't miss orders.

Can I sell to sellers outside my city?

Yes, for wholesale orders and quotes. Dropship works with sellers within 50 km or in the same city.

Can I also sell on Shopinbos as a seller?

Yes, but use a different phone number or email for your seller account.
