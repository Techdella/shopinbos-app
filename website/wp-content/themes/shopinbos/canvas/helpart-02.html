Delivery and pay on delivery

How pay on delivery works, step by step

Updated 3 days ago · 3 min read · Applies to all plans

Pay on delivery lets customers pay the rider when the parcel arrives. Shopinbos gives each order a 4-digit code, so the rider can only mark it delivered when the customer is happy.

Who can use it

All plans, for orders up to ₦50,000, in Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan and Port Harcourt. More cities are coming.

Turn on pay on delivery

In the Shopinbos app
1
Open Settings, then PaymentsTap the gear at the bottom of your Home screen.
2
Switch on Pay on deliveryChoose the most a customer can order with pay on delivery, up to ₦50,000.
3
Pick your ridersChoose Shopinbos Riders or the delivery companies you trust.
4
SaveYour checkout now shows “Pay when it arrives”.

Never share the code yourself

Only the customer should give the code to the rider. If you share it, the order can be marked delivered before the customer has the item.

Common questions

When the rider enters the customer’s 4-digit code, the payment is confirmed. It is then paid out to your bank account, usually within a few working days.
