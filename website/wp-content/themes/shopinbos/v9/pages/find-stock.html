Two ways to stock up

Dropship
No stock, no upfront cost

List supplier products in your shop. When a customer orders, the supplier packs it with your name and a rider delivers.

Wholesale
Buy in bulk, better prices

Ask suppliers for a bulk price, agree on it in the app, and get stock delivered to you.

Only sellers on Shopinbos. Shoppers never browse it. They buy from your shop, on your link.

How do I get paid?
