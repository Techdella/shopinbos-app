A **return and refund policy for online store** owners is a short page that tells customers what they can return, how long they have, who pays delivery, and how and when they get their money back. In Nigeria, where many buyers still worry about "what I ordered versus what I got", a clear policy is one of the simplest ways to build trust before someone pays.

You don't need legal language. You need plain rules that are fair to the customer, possible for you to keep, and the same every time. Below you'll find what to include, decisions to make for your type of product, how to handle refund requests calmly, and a fill-in template at the end that you can copy into your store.

## Why your online store needs a returns policy

Without a written policy, every return becomes a negotiation. One customer gets a refund, another gets store credit, a third gets nothing. Word spreads, and your shop looks unfair even when you meant well.

A clear policy helps you:

- **Win the first order.** Buyers feel safer paying a shop they haven't used before.
- **Save time.** You point to the policy instead of arguing on WhatsApp.
- **Protect your money.** You decide in advance which returns you accept.
- **Stay consistent.** Every customer gets the same treatment.
- **Handle problems calmly.** When something goes wrong, there's already a plan.

:::note Not legal advice
This guide gives practical guidance for small sellers. Consumer protection rules apply to businesses in Nigeria, so check current requirements or speak to a professional if you are unsure.
:::

## What to include in a return and refund policy

Every good policy answers the same questions. Make sure yours covers each one.

:::checklist What your policy should cover
Which items can be returned and which cannot
How many days the customer has to report a problem
How many days they have to send the item back
What condition the item must be in
How to start a return: who to contact and what to send
Who pays delivery for returns in each case
Whether you offer a refund, exchange or store credit
How and when refunds are paid
What happens with pay on delivery orders
How you handle damaged, wrong or missing items
:::

## Key decisions to make before you write

### 1. Your return window

How long does a customer have? Many small sellers use two separate time limits:

- **Time to report a problem**, such as 24 or 48 hours after delivery for damaged or wrong items.
- **Time to return for a change of mind**, such as 3 or 7 days, if you allow it at all.

Shorter windows suit fast-moving or perishable items. Longer windows suit higher-value items where customers want to be sure.

### 2. Change of mind versus your mistake

This is the most important split in any returns policy.

**When it's your mistake** (wrong item, wrong size sent, damaged, faulty, missing parts): the customer shouldn't pay anything. You cover return delivery and offer a replacement or full refund.

**When it's a change of mind** (doesn't like the colour, ordered the wrong size): you can decide whether to accept it. If you do, it's fair to ask the customer to cover return delivery and to return the item unused with tags.

### 3. Refund, exchange or store credit?

- **Refund**: money back. Best for faults, wrong items and when you can't replace.
- **Exchange**: a different size or colour. Popular in fashion.
- **Store credit**: the value to spend later. Some sellers offer it for change-of-mind returns.

Be clear which applies when. Many sellers offer a refund or exchange for their own mistakes, and an exchange or store credit for change of mind.

### 4. Items you won't take back

It's reasonable to exclude some items, as long as you say so clearly before purchase:

- Food and perishable items, unless they arrive spoiled or wrong
- Underwear, swimwear and earrings, for hygiene reasons
- Opened skincare, makeup and fragrance
- Custom or personalised items, unless there's a fault
- Items on clearance, if you state it clearly on the product

:::warn Final sale still has limits
Even on "final sale" items, if you send the wrong thing or it arrives damaged, make it right. A blanket "no returns" policy for your own mistakes damages trust fast.
:::

## Policies by product type

Different products need different rules. Here are starting points.

**Fashion and shoes.** Allow exchanges for size within a few days if unworn with tags. Include a size guide on every product to reduce returns. See [fashion selling](/solutions/fashion/).

**Beauty and skincare.** Accept returns only for sealed, unused items or for damage and wrong items. Ask for a photo on delivery if something leaked.

**Food.** Report issues on the day of delivery with photos. Offer a replacement or refund for wrong or spoiled items.

**Phones and gadgets.** Allow a short window to report faults, ask for the original box and accessories, and test returned items before refunding. State any warranty clearly.

## How refunds work for online and pay on delivery orders

### Online payments

When a customer pays online, refund them through the same channel where possible. It keeps your records clear and the customer can see the money returning to where it came from.

Tell customers:

- **How long you take to approve a refund** after receiving the item, for example 2 working days.
- **How the refund is paid**: back to the card or account they paid from, or by transfer.
- **That bank processing can add time**, outside your control.

### Pay on delivery orders

Pay on delivery changes the returns process, because the customer inspects the item when the rider arrives.

A simple approach many sellers use:

- **The customer checks the item before paying** and giving the code.
- **If it's wrong or damaged, the customer refuses** and the rider brings it back.
- **After payment, normal return rules apply.**

## Handling a return request step by step

When a customer says "this isn't what I ordered", stay calm and follow the same process every time.

:::steps Handling a return request
Thank them and ask for details | Ask for the order number, photos and a short description of the problem.
Check your records | Compare with your order and the photo you took before packing.
Decide using your policy | Confirm whether it is a fault, a wrong item or a change of mind.
Agree the solution | Offer a replacement, exchange, refund or store credit as your policy says.
Arrange the return | Book a rider or give clear instructions to send the item back.
Inspect and complete | Check the returned item, then send the refund or replacement and confirm with the customer.
:::

### Sample reply

> Hi Bola, I'm sorry the shoes came in the wrong size. Please send me a photo of the label and the order number. Since this was our mistake, we'll pick them up free and send the correct size tomorrow, or refund you in full if you prefer.

## Where to put your policy

A policy only helps if customers see it before they buy.

- **On your store**, as its own page linked from the footer and product pages.
- **In your WhatsApp Business profile** or catalog description.
- **In your Instagram highlights** under "Returns" or "Policies".
- **In your order confirmation message**, as a link.

Keep it short enough that people actually read it.

## Fill-in template: return and refund policy for your online store

Copy this into your store and replace everything in [brackets]. Delete any section that doesn't apply to you.

---

**Returns and refunds at [Your shop name]**

We want you to love what you buy. If something isn't right, here's how we'll help.

**1. Wrong, damaged or faulty items**
If your order arrives damaged, faulty, incomplete or not what you ordered, please contact us within **[48 hours]** of delivery on WhatsApp at **[phone number]** with your order number and clear photos. We'll arrange pickup at no cost to you and send a replacement or give you a full refund.

**2. Change of mind**
You can return unused items in their original packaging, with tags attached, within **[7 days]** of delivery. Return delivery for change-of-mind returns is paid by **[the customer / us]**. We offer **[an exchange / store credit / a refund]** for change-of-mind returns.

**3. Items we can't take back**
For hygiene and safety, we can't accept returns on **[food items, underwear, earrings, opened skincare and custom orders]** unless they arrive wrong, damaged or faulty.

**4. How to start a return**
Message us on **[WhatsApp number]** or email **[email address]** with your order number, the item, the reason and photos. We'll reply within **[1 working day]** with next steps.

**5. Refunds**
Once we receive and check your item, we'll approve your refund within **[2 working days]**. Refunds go back to **[the original payment method / your bank account]**. Your bank may take extra time to show the money.

**6. Pay on delivery orders**
Please check your item before you pay the rider and give your delivery code. If it's wrong or damaged, you can refuse it and we'll sort it out. After payment, the rules above apply.

**7. Exchanges**
For a different size or colour, message us within **[7 days]**. Exchanges depend on stock. If your choice is out of stock, we'll offer **[a refund / store credit]**.

**8. Questions**
We're here **[days and hours]** on **[WhatsApp number]**.

*Last updated: [date]*
