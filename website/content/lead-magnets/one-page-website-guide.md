# Building a One-Page Website for Your Business (Even If You're Not Tech-Savvy)

So you need a website for your business, but the thought of many pages and technical stuff makes your head spin? Worry less! A simple one-page website can work just as well as a complicated 20-page site.

This guide breaks it down step by step, plus a free toolkit to help you launch whenever you're ready.

## Why One-Page Websites Work for Small Businesses

People want information fast, especially on their phones. A one-page website gives visitors everything they need without clicking through many pages.

Whether you make cakes in Ibadan, sell ankara in Aba or offer hair services in Abuja, a one-page site lets you tell your story clearly and guide visitors to one action, like sending you a WhatsApp message or buying your product.

It is also easier to keep up to date. No broken links or forgotten pages, just one page that stays current.

## What Your One-Page Website Needs

Every good one-page site has the same basic sections:

- Header with your business name and contact details
- A short introduction to what you do
- Your main products or services
- Why customers should choose you
- A clear contact section or button (for example "Chat on WhatsApp")

**Quick test:** If someone spends 30 seconds on your page, will they understand what you do and how to reach you?

## Planning Your Content

Spend 20 minutes writing down what you want to say. Answer these questions:

- What problem do you solve for customers?
- What makes you different?
- What should visitors do after reading your page?

Keep your answers simple and friendly. Write like you're talking to a customer in your shop.

## Design Basics That Look Professional

Simple usually looks more professional than busy.

**Focus on:**

- Plenty of empty space so it's easy to read
- 2–3 colours at most
- One clear, easy-to-read font
- Phone number or WhatsApp button visible without scrolling
- Clear, bright photos of your real products

## Writing Words That Connect

Talk about how you help customers, not everything about your business.

Instead of: *"We provide comprehensive beauty solutions"*
Say: *"Natural shea butter and hair oils, delivered to your door anywhere in Lagos."*

**The 3-second rule:** Someone should understand what you do within 3 seconds of opening your page.

## Essential Sections in Order

Arrange your page like a story:

1. **Header:** Business name, short tagline, contact details
2. **Top banner:** One clear sentence about what you do
3. **About:** A short story about you and your business
4. **Products or services:** Main offers with simple descriptions and prices in naira
5. **Reviews:** Customer comments or screenshots of happy WhatsApp messages (with permission)
6. **Contact:** WhatsApp, phone, Instagram and your address or delivery areas

Keep each section short. People scan websites rather than reading every word.

## Getting Your Website Online

Most website builders handle the technical side for you.

**Simple steps:**

- Choose a short domain name (.com.ng or .ng are good for Nigerian businesses)
- Connect the domain to your website builder
- Check that everything works on an Android phone
- Test your WhatsApp link, contact form and payment buttons (Paystack or Flutterwave payment links work well)

Use your business name for your domain if it's available.

## Help People Find You

- Add your business to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/en-all/business-profile/) (free and important)
- Mention your city and area naturally in your text, for example "Lekki, Lagos"
- Use the words your customers would search for
- Make sure your site loads fast, even on slow mobile data
