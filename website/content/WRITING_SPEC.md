# Shopinbos content: facts and style (read before writing)

Shopinbos (shopinbos.com) is an app for Nigerian businesses to sell online and reach customers: find products to sell,
build a store, sell everywhere (own store, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Google), run orders and stock,
talk to customers, deliver and get paid. Shopinbos is a product of Tech Della Solutions Ltd.

Facts you may use (do not invent others):
- Free plan (up to 20 products, 3% fee on online sales). Paid plans from ₦6,000 a month (Starter), ₦18,000 (Growth), ₦40,000 (Pro), in naira.
- Rex = the AI store builder (builds your store from a chat, about 5 minutes). Peggy = AI assistant that answers customer chats
  (English and Pidgin) and drafts posts.
- Payments through Paystack: card, bank transfer, USSD. Fee 1.5% + ₦100 (₦100 waived under ₦2,500), capped at ₦2,000.
- Pay on delivery: orders up to ₦50,000 in Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan and Port Harcourt. Each order has a 4-digit code the customer
  gives the rider. Riders pay cash in by 6pm the same day.
- Payments settle straight to the seller’s own bank, usually the next working day. Shopinbos never holds sales money: no balance of sales, no payout day, no withdraw. Cash on delivery is paid to the seller directly. The prepaid wallet balance is spend-only (AI credits and other Shopinbos services).
- Riders bookable from the order; delivery price shown at checkout; interstate via delivery partner companies.
- Find stock: checked Nigerian suppliers; dropship (supplier ships with your shop's label) or buy wholesale; you are paid after delivery.
- Store at yourname.shopinbos.com free; connect your own domain on paid plans.
- Support on WhatsApp, 8am to 8pm, English or Pidgin.

Never invent statistics, studies, survey results, quotes from real people, or exact prices/fees of other companies or
government agencies. Say "check the current fee on the CAC portal", "many sellers find", etc. Example sellers must be
labelled as examples. No claims about competitors you can't support; be fair.

Style: plain, warm UK/Nigerian English, short sentences and paragraphs (2-4 sentences), second person, practical steps,
naira examples (₦), Nigerian context (Lagos, Abuja, Aba, Kano, Computer Village, Balogun, Status, Pidgin phrases sparingly).
No em dashes. No hype words (revolutionary, game-changer, unlock, leverage, seamless). Sentence-case headings.
