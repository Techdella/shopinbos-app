What do I need to register as a rider?

A valid ID, a working bike or vehicle, a smartphone and a Nigerian bank account. [Confirm final requirements.]

How does pay on delivery work for riders?

Collect the payment, then enter the customer’s 4-digit code to confirm the handover. Everything is tracked in the app.

When do I get paid?

Instantly, to your bank account, once the delivery is confirmed.

Can my company cover more than one state?

Yes. Add every area you cover and set prices for each.
