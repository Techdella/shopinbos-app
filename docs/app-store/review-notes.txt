Notes
Shopinbos is a store builder for small businesses in Nigeria and other African countries. Sellers build an online
shop, add products from photos, answer customer chats, record orders and arrange delivery of physical goods.

- Payments in the app are for physical goods and services that sellers sell to their own customers (Paystack, outside
  the app), or for deliveries done by delivery companies (guideline 3.1.3(e)).
- The app is a free stand-alone companion to Shopinbos, a paid web-based business tool (guideline 3.1.3(f)). It has
  no purchasing inside the app and no calls to action to buy elsewhere: plans and AI credits can't be bought, priced
  or linked to from the iOS app, including on the few website tools it opens.
- Sign in with Apple is offered next to Google sign-in. Phone sign-in uses SMS codes.
- Account deletion: the "My shop" tab › "Delete my account" (under "Log out"); suppliers and delivery partners have it
  under More.
- Microphone: only when the person taps the mic to speak, or calls Peggy (the AI assistant, a voice call).
- Location: only for delivery partners while they are online or on a delivery (when in use, never in the background).
- Camera: product photos, proof of delivery photos, and a selfie for delivery partners' identity checks.
- Suppliers and delivery companies use the same app with their own accounts; the demo account is a seller.
