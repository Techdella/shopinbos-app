Here is **how to sell on TikTok in Nigeria** in one paragraph: switch to a business account, add a link to a store or WhatsApp in your bio, post short videos that show your product in real use, reply to every "how much?" comment, and send interested viewers to a place where they can pay and book delivery. Post often, learn from what gets comments, and repeat what works.

TikTok can put your product in front of thousands of people who have never heard of you, even with zero followers. That is its big strength. The challenge is turning views into orders, and that is what most of this guide is about.

## Why TikTok is worth it for Nigerian sellers

On most social apps, people see posts from accounts they follow. On TikTok, the For You page shows videos to people based on what they watch, so a new seller can reach new buyers quickly if a video catches attention.

That makes TikTok good for:

- **Finding new customers** outside your contacts and followers
- **Showing products in action**, which builds trust faster than photos
- **Testing ideas**, because you see quickly which products people react to
- **Building a brand** with a face and a voice people recognise

The flip side is that views are not orders. A video can get many views and zero sales if viewers cannot work out how to buy or do not trust you yet.

TikTok Shop is not open to sellers in every country. Check inside the app whether it is available to you. Most Nigerian sellers sell by sending viewers to a store link or WhatsApp, which is what this guide covers.
:::

## Step 1: set up your TikTok account for selling

A few minutes of setup makes your account look like a real business and makes ordering easier.

:::steps Account setup
Switch to a business account | In settings, choose the option to switch to a business account so you get analytics and business tools.
Pick a clear name and handle | Use your shop name, and keep it the same as your Instagram if you can.
Write a short bio | Say what you sell, where you deliver and how to order, like "Wigs and hair care. Lagos and nationwide. Order below."
Add your link | Add your store or WhatsApp link in the website field so viewers can order in one tap.
Add a profile photo | Use your logo or a clear photo of you, since faces build trust on TikTok.
:::

If you do not see the website field, check that you are on a business account. TikTok sometimes changes which accounts can add links, so look in your profile settings.

## Step 2: choose what to sell on TikTok

Some products are made for short video. They have a visible "before and after", a satisfying moment, or a clear problem they solve.

Products that often do well:

- **Beauty and hair:** wig installs, skincare routines, lash application
- **Fashion:** outfit changes, styling one piece three ways, try-on hauls
- **Gadgets:** unboxings, "this charger saved me during NEPA", sound tests
- **Food:** making small chops, cutting a cake, packing snack boxes
- **Home and kitchen:** gadgets that solve a small annoying problem

If you are not sure what to sell, browse the [trending products page](/trending/) for ideas, then test a few with videos before buying stock.

## Step 3: make videos that sell (no dancing required)

You do not need to dance, act or be famous. You need to show the product clearly and give people a reason to want it.

### Seven video ideas that work

1. **"Unbox with me":** open a new delivery and show each item close up.
2. **"Pack an order with me":** shows you are real, busy and trusted.
3. **Before and after:** hair, skin, a room, a messy drawer.
4. **"3 ways to wear":** one scarf, one dress, one bag.
5. **Answer a comment:** reply to "how much?" or "does it come in black?" with a video.
6. **Problem and fix:** "My phone always dies at 3pm. This is what I carry now."
7. **Customer reaction:** with permission, show a happy customer receiving their order.

### Hook viewers in the first two seconds

Start with the product or the result, not your logo or "Hi guys". For example:

- Show the finished wig, then explain how.
- Hold the blender full of pepper, then press the button.
- Start with "This bag fits a 15-inch laptop" while you slide the laptop in.

### Keep it simple to film

Use your phone, natural light from a window and a quiet spot. Clear sound matters more than a fancy camera. Many sellers film several videos in one sitting and post them through the week.

:::tip Say the price in the video
Put the price on screen or say it out loud. "₦22,000, delivered in Lagos tomorrow" answers the question most viewers have and filters out people who were never going to buy.
:::

### Write captions and use hashtags well

TikTok captions are short, so make them count.

- Say what the product is in plain words: "Human hair bob wig, 12 inches"
- Add the price and a call to action: "₦65,000. Order via link in bio"
- Use a few relevant hashtags: your product, your city and your niche
- Ask a question to get comments: "Black or brown?"

Avoid stuffing dozens of hashtags. A few relevant ones help TikTok understand your video and show it to the right people.

## Step 4: turn comments and DMs into orders

This is where most TikTok sellers lose sales. A viewer comments "how much?", nobody replies, and they forget.

:::checklist Comment and DM routine
Reply to every buying question within a few hours
Pin a comment with the price and how to order
Reply to common questions with a video
Move serious buyers to your store link or WhatsApp
Follow up once if someone asked but did not order
:::

## Step 5: go live to sell more

TikTok Live lets you talk to buyers in real time, show products up close and answer questions immediately. Live access depends on TikTok's current rules for your account, so check in the app whether you can go live.

Tips for a good selling live:

- Plan five to ten products to show, in order
- Have prices on a card or on screen
- Hold each product close to the camera and show details
- Read out names of people asking questions and answer them
- Tell viewers exactly how to order: "Tap the link in bio, choose the colour, pay or pick pay on delivery"
- Go live at times your buyers are free, such as evenings and weekends

## Mistakes that keep TikTok views from becoming sales

- **No link in bio.** Viewers who want to buy do not know where to go.
- **Ignoring comments.** Each unanswered "how much?" is a lost sale.
- **Only posting adverts.** Mix in behind the scenes, tips and reviews.
- **Giving up after a week.** Growth on TikTok is uneven. Some videos flop and one suddenly takes off.
- **Selling something you cannot restock.** A video goes viral, and you have three pieces left. Know your supplier before you push a product.
- **Copying videos exactly.** Use trends for ideas, but show your own products, voice and city.
