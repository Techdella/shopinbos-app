Talk to us
Do I sell to shoppers directly?

No. On Find stock you sell to shops. They sell to their own customers under their own names.

How do I get paid?

To your bank, through Paystack, usually within a few working days.

Who delivers?

A rider or delivery company picks up from you. Delivery is booked in the app.

Can shops ask for bulk prices?

Yes. Shops can send price requests, and you reply with your best offer.
