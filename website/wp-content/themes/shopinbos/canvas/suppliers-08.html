About selling wholesale online

Many Nigerian makers and wholesalers already sell to resellers on WhatsApp. Shopinbos turns those resellers into a steady channel: they sell your products in their own stores, and you get orders without chasing anyone for payment.

Because every shop and every supplier is checked, and each payment goes straight to the right bank, both sides can trust the deal. It works for dropshipping, bulk wholesale and custom quotes.
