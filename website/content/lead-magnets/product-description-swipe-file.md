# Your Product Description and Copywriting Swipe File (Starter Pack)

Feeling stuck every time you write a product description? You are not alone. For many new sellers, a blank page is the biggest problem. This starter pack helps you build a "swipe file": a collection of ready templates you can adapt for any product in minutes, for your store, Instagram captions or WhatsApp catalogue.

## Part 1: The Mindset Shift – What Makes a Product Description Sell?

Before the templates, understand the goal. A good product description does not just describe; it persuades. It answers the customer's biggest question: "What is in it for me?"

Do not just list features (for example, "made of cotton"). Focus on benefits (for example, "Soft, breathable cotton that keeps you cool even in Lagos heat"). Show how the product makes your customer's life better.

## Part 2: Your "Fill-in-the-Blanks" Swipe File Templates

Copy, paste and adapt these templates. They are your secret weapon for writing descriptions that make people buy.

### Template A: The Problem-Solver

*(Perfect for products that fix a common problem)*

- Headline: Tired of \[customer's problem\]?
- Intro: Imagine \[the result they want\]. With \[product name\], it is finally possible.
- How it works: We made it with \[key feature\] so you can \[key benefit\].
- Call to action: Stop struggling. Order now, or message us on WhatsApp.

*Example:* Tired of dry, breaking edges? Halima's unrefined shea butter from Kano keeps your hair moisturised all day.

### Template B: The Storyteller

*(Ideal for fashion, lifestyle and gift items)*

- Headline: \[A phrase that creates a feeling, for example "Owambe Ready"\].
- The story: Picture this: \[a short scene where the product is used\]. That is the feeling you get with \[product name\].
- Why it is for you: Made for \[your target customer\], it helps you \[reach a goal or feel a certain way\].
- Call to action: Ready to make your own memories? Order yours today.

*Example:* Picture this: you walk into your cousin's wedding in Ibadan in Adaeze's ankara wrap gown, and every head turns.

### Template C: The Straight-Shooter

*(Best for practical items and price-conscious customers)*

- Headline: The \[product category\] you can rely on.
- What it is: \[Product name\] gives you \[main benefit 1\] and \[main benefit 2\] without stress.
- Key details:
  - Get \[benefit\] because of \[feature\].
  - Enjoy \[benefit\] because of \[feature\].
- Call to action: For a reliable, no-nonsense choice, tap "Add to cart".

*Example:* The power bank you can rely on: charges your phone up to four times, even when there is no light.

## Part 3: Words to "Swipe" and Use Instantly

Build a word bank! Here are useful words grouped by the feeling they create.

- For comfort and quality: Soft, smooth, durable, sturdy, premium, neat, well-finished, luxurious.
- For results: Powerful, long-lasting, visible, fast-acting, tested, original, game-changing.
- For exclusivity and urgency: Limited, exclusive, handpicked, bestselling, new arrival, last few pieces, Detty December special.

Only say "guaranteed" or "original" if it is true. Trust is everything.

## Part 4: Your 3-Step Action Plan

Do not get lost in theory. Follow these steps this week.

1. Gather inspiration (15 mins): Look at 3 brands you admire on Instagram, Jumia or Konga. Copy 2-3 of their best descriptions into your phone notes or a Google Doc. Write down what you like about them.
2. Analyse and "swipe" (20 mins): Which template (A, B or C) does each one look like? Pick out the strong words and add them to your word bank.
3. Write your first description (25 mins): Pick one of your products, choose the best template and fill in the blanks. Do not aim for perfection. Just get a draft done!

## Your Takeaway

Your swipe file is a living document. The more you add, the more useful it becomes. Every time a description or caption makes you want to buy, "swipe" it, study it and adapt it. This habit will quickly build your confidence and turn writing into your new superpower.
