Talk to us
Do I need a website or a laptop?

No. Run everything from your phone. Your shop gets a free yourshop.mybos.store address, and you can connect your own domain later.

Is Shopinbos a marketplace?

No. Customers buy from your own shop, on your link, under your name. Find stock is a private catalogue for sellers to source from suppliers. Shoppers never browse it.

When do I get paid?

Payments are paid out to your bank account through Paystack, usually within a few working days. You can see every payment and fee in the app.

How does pay on delivery work?

When the parcel arrives, your customer pays you from their link or the rider’s QR code. Then they get a 4-digit code, the rider types it in, and the app shows Paid before the parcel is handed over.

Can I sell without buying stock?

Yes. Add products from suppliers on Shopinbos to your shop. When a customer orders, the supplier packs it with your shop’s name and a rider delivers it.

Moving from another platform?

Bring your products across and keep your domain. We’ll help you on WhatsApp.
