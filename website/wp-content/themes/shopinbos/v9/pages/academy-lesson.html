Turn on pay on delivery

NotesYour taskTranscript

Many Nigerian shoppers want to see their order before they pay. Pay on delivery lets them do that, and the 4-digit code keeps you safe.

1Your customer chooses pay on delivery at checkout and gets a 4-digit code.
2The rider collects the payment at the door.
3The customer gives the rider the code only when the order is in their hands.
4The rider enters the code, and the order is marked delivered and paid.
Your task
Go to Settings → Payments and switch on pay on delivery for the areas you deliver to.
