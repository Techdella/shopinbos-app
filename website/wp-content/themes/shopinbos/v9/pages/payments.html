When do I get my money?

Instantly. Money goes straight to your bank as each order is paid.

Can I see every payment?

Yes. Every payment, fee and payout is listed in Money, with a monthly statement.

What does it cost?

A small fee per successful payment. You see it on every payment. [Confirm rates.]

How does pay on delivery stay safe?

The customer gets a 4-digit code and only gives it to the rider when the order is in their hands. The payment and handover are recorded in the app.
