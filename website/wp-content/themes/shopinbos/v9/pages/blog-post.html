How to price your products in naira, without guessing

Shopinbos team
Guides for sellers
On this pageWork out your real costAdd delivery and feesCheck the marketPick your priceTest and adjust
Share on WhatsAppCopy link

Pricing is where most new sellers lose money without noticing. Here’s a simple way to set a price that covers your costs and still feels fair to your customer.

1. Work out your real cost

Start with what you paid for the item. Then add everything else it took to get it ready to sell: transport to the market, packaging, a share of your data for posting, and anything you had to throw away. That number is your real cost.

Example · Glow skincare kit
What you paid₦9,500
Transport and packaging₦900
Payment fee [x]%₦[fee]
Real cost₦10,400 + fee

2. Add delivery and fees

Decide whether delivery is included or added at checkout. If you include it, add an average delivery cost to every product. In Shopinbos, the delivery price is shown at checkout, so most sellers keep it separate.

Tip from Peggy: ask “What did I spend to get this into a customer’s hands?” If you can’t answer quickly, your price is a guess.

3. Check the market

Look at what similar shops charge, but don’t race to the bottom. Customers pay more for good photos, quick replies and delivery they can trust.

4. Pick your price

Add the profit you want on top of your real cost. Round to a clean number, like ₦18,500 instead of ₦18,370.

5. Test and adjust

Watch how fast it sells for two weeks. Selling out in a day? You can probably charge more. Not moving? Check your photos and description before you cut the price.
