01 · About

What Shopinbos is

Shopinbos is an AI commerce platform for African businesses. Sellers build online stores with our AI assistant Chip, source stock from suppliers, and sell to customers across Nigeria. Every one of those orders needs to be delivered, and that's where you come in.

Shopinbos is the software in the middle. We don't carry parcels ourselves. We connect shops and suppliers to verified delivery partners like you, price delivery at checkout from your rate card, send the job to your team, and pay your share of the delivery fee straight to your bank.

Steady jobs

Orders from Shopinbos stores reach you automatically once a shop connects with you.

Your prices

You set your own rate card by route, service and parcel size. Shops see your prices at checkout.

Paid fast

Your share of each in-app delivery fee settles to your bank, usually the next working day.

Where we operateShopinbos delivery currently runs in Nigeria, in all 36 states and the FCT. All prices are in Naira (₦).
02 · The big picture

How delivery works on Shopinbos

From sign-up to your first payout, the journey looks like this.

  1. You sign up and set up your companyTell us where you deliver, what vehicles you run and how fast you deliver.
  2. You get verifiedUpload your CAC and insurance documents. Shops only see verified partners, and jobs only come once you're verified.
  3. You publish a rate cardYour prices for each route, service and parcel size. You need a public rate card to show up when shops search for partners.
  4. Shops connect with youA shop finds you in the delivery directory and adds you as a partner. They sign a delivery service agreement, and you sign it too under Contracts.
  5. Customers check outYour delivery price shows at checkout. When the order is paid (or placed as pay-on-delivery), a job is created and sent to you.
  6. You accept, pick up and deliverAssign a rider, collect with the shop's pickup code and deliver with the customer's delivery code.
  7. You get paidWhen a customer pays by card, your share of the delivery fee is split directly to your bank account through Paystack.

Jobs come from three places

  • Store checkout: customers pick your service when they buy from a shop you're connected to.
  • Dropship orders: parcels are booked with you when a supplier packs a dropship order.
  • Direct bookings & quotes: sellers can book you directly or send a quote request for a custom route.
03 · Sign-up

Signing up, step by step

Go to app.shopinbos.com/logistics/signup. You can sign up with your phone number or email (we send a one-time code by WhatsApp, or by SMS if WhatsApp isn't available) or with Google. No password needed.

Once you're in, a short setup wizard walks you through six steps. You can come back and change everything later.

  1. CompanyYour company name, city and a short "about" for your profile.
  2. CoverageThe regions and states you deliver in, and a starting price for each zone (we suggest ₦900). You can pause a zone at any time.
  3. FleetYour vehicle types (bikevantruck) and delivery types (same-dayexpressnext-daybulk).
  4. PricingYour base price and delivery speed, e.g. "Same-day in active zones" or "45–90 minutes in active zones".
  5. VerifyAn instant check of your CAC registration (RC or BN number). You can skip this and upload documents later.
  6. ReviewCheck everything and submit. Your account moves to pending while our team reviews it.
Minimum to submitAt least one active coverage zone, one vehicle type and one delivery type. Your Free plan covers up to 3 states (see section 09).

Your setup checklist

Your dashboard shows a checklist until you're fully set up:

Company details→Where you deliver→Pickup points→Rates→Verification documents→Bank for payments→Verified by Shopinbos

No CAC registration yet?

You can register a business name with the Corporate Affairs Commission through Shopinbos at /logistics/register-business. It costs ₦32,000 all-in, which includes the ₦27,000 CAC fee.

05 · Where & how much

Coverage, rates & pickup points

Coverage

Under Coverage you list the services you offer and the areas you collect from and deliver to.

Areas can be as broad or as narrow as you like: a whole region ("South-West"), a state ("Lagos"), a single LGA ("Lagos: Ikeja"), or "Nationwide". All 774 LGAs are supported.

Your rate card

Your rate card is what shops and customers pay. Each row is one route and service:

Parcel sizes

  • Small: up to 2 kg
  • Medium: 2–10 kg
  • Bulky: over 10 kg

A parcel can use its own size's price or a bigger size's price, never a smaller one.

Which price is used?

The most specific match wins: an LGA beats a state, a state beats a region, and a region beats Nationwide. If two rows tie, the cheaper one is used.

  • Up to 300 rows per rate card; delivery times up to 60 days. Same-day rows must be 0 days.
  • Optional extra charges: a failed-attempt / re-attempt fee and a return-to-sender fee.
  • Saving a new rate card replaces the old one. Orders already placed keep the price they were placed at.
  • Rex, our AI assistant, can build a rate card from your existing price list (10 AI credits).

Private rates

On Logistics Plus and Pro you can send a private rate card to a single business under Connections. It applies once that business accepts.

Pickup points

If customers can collect parcels from your offices or agent locations, add them under Pickup points: name, address, state, area, opening hours, phone and map pin. The number you can add depends on your plan (3 on Free, 20 on Plus, unlimited on Pro).

06 · Your team

Adding riders & your team

Add your riders and office staff under Riders in your portal. Riders work through the Shopinbos Riders app; office staff work in the partner portal.

RoleWhat they do
RiderPicks up and delivers jobs using the Riders app.
DispatcherAccepts jobs, assigns riders, updates statuses.
ManagerRuns the account: rates, coverage, team, money.
SupportHandles messages and customer issues.

How to add a rider

  1. Open Riders → Add riderEnter their name, phone number, plate number and preferred language: English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo.
  2. Set their payPer drop (a fixed ₦ amount per delivered job) or Share (a % of your fee after any Shopinbos fee). Each delivery keeps the pay rate it was done at.
  3. Send them their Riders app linkEach rider gets a private link you can copy or share on WhatsApp. The link is their login, so no password is needed. Making a new link switches off the old one. Riders can also sign in with their phone number and a 6-digit code.

Office staff (dispatchers, managers, support) get an email invite instead.

What riders can do in the app

  • Go online and offline
  • See and update their jobs
  • Enter pickup and delivery codes
  • Skip a job (before pickup only)
  • Report a problem ("Something wrong?")
  • See this week's earnings

Rider location & privacy

A rider's location is only shared while they're online or on a job, and only with your company. It is never shown to shops or customers. Each job's location trail is deleted 7 days after delivery.

Team size by planFree: up to 3 team members · Logistics Plus: 10 · Logistics Pro: unlimited. Riders join as part of a registered delivery company; individual riders can't sign up on their own.
07 · Day to day

Handling jobs

New jobs land on your Today screen and under Jobs. Accept or decline, assign a rider, and keep the status up to date so the shop and customer know where their parcel is.

The life of a job

Pending→Confirmed→Picked up→In transit→Out for delivery→Delivered

Jobs can also go to At pickup point, Failed, Returning → Returned, Declined or Cancelled. A failed job can be re-attempted.

Time limits

  • Accept within 4 hours. After that the job is flagged to the shop as late.
  • Pick up within 24 hours of the shop marking the parcel ready.
  • A delivery counts as on time if it arrives within 12 hours of the promised date.

Codes & proof

  • Pickup code: a 4-digit code from the shop, given to your rider at collection.
  • Delivery code: a 4-digit code from the customer, needed to mark a job delivered.
  • A proof photo is optional.

Declining a job

If you can't take a job, decline it with a reason so the shop can send it to another partner quickly:

  • We don't cover this route right now
  • No rider free in time
  • The parcel is too big for us
  • The address is incomplete
  • Other

Failed deliveries

Every failed delivery needs a reason, e.g. customer didn't pick up calls, not at the address, refused the parcel, wrong or incomplete address, or couldn't pay. If a delivery fails or is refused through no fault of yours, the shop still pays for it, and your failed-attempt and return fees apply.

Quote requests

Sellers can ask you to price a custom route. Reply under Quote requests with a price, a note and a "good until" date, or decline.

Customer updates

If the shop turns them on, customers get automatic WhatsApp/SMS updates when the job is booked, out for delivery (with their delivery code), ready at a pickup point, delivered, or if there's a problem.

08 · Money in

Getting paid

Shopinbos never holds your money. When a customer pays for delivery by card, the payment is split at the moment of payment: your share goes straight to your bank account through Paystack.

How a delivery fee is paid out

Customer pays at checkout→Paystack splits the payment→Delivery fee to your bank

Money usually reaches your bank the next working day. Paystack's processing fee comes out of your share. Shares under ₦100 can't be split.

Set up your wallet (one time)

To be paid in the app, complete Wallet setup:

  1. Confirm your identityYour BVN or NIN (11 digits) and date of birth. You must be 18 or older. We only keep the last 4 digits.
  2. Add your bank accountThe account name must include your first and last name. Company accounts are reviewed by our team.
No wallet yet?If your wallet isn't ready, the delivery fee stays with the shop and the shop pays you directly, either in the app by card or offline. Set up your wallet early so you're paid automatically.

Your Earnings screen

See what you've earned today and over the last 30 days, what's on the road now, and any Shopinbos fees, all under Earnings. Fees lists every job and its fee.

10 · Pay on delivery

Cash on delivery

Pay-on-delivery (POD) is off unless the shop, the connection and you all turn it on. You choose whether to allow it overall, and per shop under Connections.

The cash belongs to the shop

Shopinbos never collects POD cash. Your rider collects it from the customer (cash or transfer) and records the amount and method when marking the job delivered. You then pass the money to the shop.

Passing cash to the shop

Pay each shop in the app (split straight to the shop's wallet), or pay them directly and mark it cash paid to shop. The shop confirms, and the order is marked paid. Track it all under Cash on delivery.

Deadline: 6pm the same day

Cash collected before 6pm (Lagos time) must be passed to the shop by 6pm that day. Cash collected after 6pm is due by 6pm the next day. You'll get a reminder 2 hours before the deadline, and there's a 60-minute grace period.

If cash is late

WhenWhat happens
After the deadlineYou're paused from new POD jobs. Prepaid jobs continue as normal.
Each late dayA strike: −5 points on your reliability score, which lowers your place in search.

If you pay a shop more than you owe, the extra is kept as credit.

11 · Being found

How shops find you

Shops find delivery partners in the Shopinbos delivery directory, both in the app and on shopinbos.com. You show up when you're verified, active, and have a public rate card that covers the shop's area.

What moves you up the list

  • Being verified (the biggest boost)
  • A good route match for the shop's area
  • Volume: more completed jobs, up to 1,000
  • On-time rate
  • Your rating
  • Low failed-delivery rate (failures pull you down)

On-time and failed rates are shown once you've finished at least 5 jobs. In the directory, Pro partners are listed first, then Plus, then Free. Featured boosts put you at the top in the states you choose.

When a shop connects with you

Connecting is instant on your public rates. The shop signs a Delivery service agreement, and you sign or decline it under Contracts. In short:

  • You're responsible for parcels from collection until delivery, including loss or damage.
  • Disputes are decided by Shopinbos using what the app recorded (statuses, codes, timestamps).
  • Customer data must be handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
  • Either side can end the connection at any time.

Each shop chooses which of your services customers see (doorstep, pickup point), whether to show your company name, and whether to allow POD. Shops can also send you direct bookings and quote requests.

Make your profile shine

Add your logo, a cover image (Rex can make one), opening hours and same-day cut-off under Company profile.

12 · Good standing

Claims, rules & good standing

Lost or damaged parcels

  • Shops can claim for a parcel that was lost or damaged in transit within 7 days of delivery, up to the value of the order (minimum ₦100).
  • An accepted claim must be paid within 3 days.
  • If it isn't, up to 50% of each later in-app delivery fee goes to the shop until the claim is covered.
  • If a claim is 7 days overdue, your account is paused so new shops can't find you.

Customer issues

Customers can report a parcel as late, not delivered, damaged or the wrong item. You're notified of late, not-delivered and damaged reports so you can sort them out quickly.

Keep jobs and payments in Shopinbos

ImportantPayments arranged outside the app aren't protected by Shopinbos. The inbox will flag messages that share phone numbers, bank details or "pay me directly" requests. Keep jobs and money in the app so you're covered if anything goes wrong.

Your obligations at a glance

  • Hold a valid CAC registration and goods-in-transit insurance.
  • Make sure your riders' papers are checked and in order.
  • Accept or decline jobs within 4 hours, and pick up within 24 hours.
  • Pass POD cash to shops by 6pm.
  • Treat customer details as private and use them only to deliver.

The full Delivery Partner Terms are on shopinbos.com. Legal questions: legal@shopinbos.com.

14 · FAQ

Questions & support

Does it cost anything to join?

No. The Free (Verified) plan is ₦0, and the job fee is 0% at launch.

How long does verification take?

Our team reviews submissions as they come in. Upload clear documents and pass the instant CAC check to speed things up.

Can a single rider join without a company?

Not at the moment. Riders join as team members of a registered delivery company.

Who pays for a failed delivery?

If it wasn't your fault (customer not home, refused the parcel, wrong address), the shop still pays, plus any failed-attempt or return fee on your rate card.

Can I see where my riders are?

Yes. You see riders who are online or on a job. Shops and customers never see rider locations.

Can I pause taking jobs?

Yes. Switch off "Taking new jobs" on your Today screen. You'll still finish jobs you already have.

Can I change my prices?

Any time. Orders already placed keep the price they were placed at.
