If you're wondering **how to register a business name in Nigeria**, the process happens online through the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). You create an account on the CAC portal, search that your chosen name is available, fill in your details and the nature of your business, upload the documents it asks for, pay the fee online and wait for approval. Once approved, you download your certificate.

For an online seller, that certificate is more than paperwork. It helps you open a business bank account, makes customers more comfortable paying you, and prepares you for bigger supplier and corporate orders. This guide explains what to prepare, how the steps usually go, and what to do once you're registered. Fees and screens can change, so always check the current details on the CAC portal itself.

## Why online sellers should register a business name

Plenty of people sell successfully on WhatsApp Status and Instagram without registering. So why bother?

- **Customer trust.** When a buyer transfers money, they see the account name. "Chioma's Bags Enterprises" feels safer than a personal name they don't recognise.
- **A business bank account.** Banks usually ask for registration documents to open an account in your business name.
- **Separating money.** A business account keeps sales away from personal spending, so you can see your real profit.
- **Bigger opportunities.** Some suppliers, corporate buyers and partners prefer to deal with registered businesses.
- **Protecting your name.** Registering stops someone else registering the same business name.

:::note Not legal advice
This guide explains the general process for online sellers. For your specific situation, especially if you have partners or plan to raise money, speak to an accredited agent or lawyer.
:::

## Business name or limited company?

CAC registers different types of businesses. The two most small sellers think about are:

### Business name

Also called a sole proprietorship or partnership. It's registered under your own name as the owner, trading under a business name. It's usually the simpler and cheaper option, and it's what most new online sellers start with.

**Good for:** solo sellers and small partnerships who want to make their business official without much complexity.

### Private limited company (Ltd)

A separate legal entity from you. It has more requirements, such as directors, shareholders and ongoing filings, but it separates your personal liability from the business.

**Good for:** businesses with several owners, plans to raise investment, or larger contracts.

Many sellers start with a business name, then incorporate a limited company as they grow. This guide focuses on business name registration.

## What to prepare before you start

Having everything ready makes the process smoother. Requirements can change, so check the CAC portal, but sellers are usually asked for items like these.

:::checklist Prepare before you register
Two or three business name options, in order of preference
A short description of what your business does
Your business address, with a landmark
Your residential address
A valid means of identification
A recent passport photograph in digital form
Your signature, scanned or photographed clearly
A working email address and phone number
A way to pay the fee online
:::

### Choosing a good business name

Your name should work on a certificate, a bank account, an Instagram handle and a store link. Some tips:

- **Keep it short and easy to spell.** People will type it into search bars and WhatsApp.
- **Check social media and domains** before you register, so you can use the same name everywhere.
- **Avoid names too close to existing brands.** CAC may reject them, and it can cause trouble later.
- **Avoid restricted words** unless you meet the requirements. Words that suggest government links or regulated activities may need extra approval.
- **Think ahead.** "Ada's Ankara" is fine, but if you plan to sell shoes and bags later, "Ada's Closet" may fit better.

:::tip Match your store link
If your registered name is "Kemi Beauty Hub", aim for a store at kemibeautyhub.shopinbos.com and the same handle on Instagram and TikTok. Consistency builds trust.
:::

## How to register a business name in Nigeria: step by step

Here's the general flow on the CAC online portal. The screens and labels may look slightly different when you do it, but the steps are similar.

:::steps Register your business name with CAC
Create a portal account | Go to the official CAC website, open the registration portal and sign up with your email and phone.
Check name availability | Search your preferred name to see if it is available and acceptable.
Reserve the name | Submit your chosen name for reservation if the portal asks you to.
Fill in business details | Enter the business name, nature of business, business address and start date.
Add proprietor details | Enter your personal details as the owner, and those of any partners.
Upload documents | Upload your ID, passport photo and signature in the format the portal requests.
Pay the fee online | Check the current fee shown on the CAC portal and pay through the portal.
Track and download | Monitor your application status and download your certificate once approved.
:::

### Step 1: Create your account

Use the official CAC website. Be careful of look-alike sites or social media pages that offer "registration" and ask you to pay into personal accounts. Use an email you check often, because updates and queries are sent there.

### Step 2: Search and reserve your name

Enter your first-choice name. If it's not available or is too similar to another, try your second option. Some names are rejected for being too general, misleading, or close to existing businesses.

### Step 3: Describe your business

You'll usually choose or describe the nature of your business. Be clear and accurate. For example: "Online retail of clothing, bags and fashion accessories" or "Sale of baked goods and snacks".

### Step 4: Enter your details and upload documents

Fill in your personal details exactly as they appear on your ID. Mismatched spellings are a common reason for delays. Upload clear, readable scans or photos.

### Step 5: Pay and submit

The portal will show the current fee. **Check the current fee on the CAC portal** rather than relying on figures you see on social media, which may be out of date. Pay through the official portal only.

### Step 6: Wait for approval

CAC reviews your application. If they need changes, you'll get a query. Respond quickly and correctly. Once approved, you can download your certificate and related documents from your account.

:::warn Avoid registration scams
Only pay through the official CAC portal or a properly accredited agent. Don't send money to someone on WhatsApp who promises "fast CAC" with no proof of accreditation.
:::

## Doing it yourself or using an agent

You can register a business name yourself online. Many sellers do. But some prefer an accredited agent.

**Do it yourself if:**
- You're comfortable filling forms online
- You have all your documents ready
- You want to save the agent's fee

**Use an accredited agent if:**
- You've had queries or rejections before
- You don't have time to follow up
- You're also registering a company or need advice on structure

If you use an agent, confirm they're accredited, ask for a clear breakdown of what you're paying for, and make sure the registration is in your name with your details.

## After registration: what to do next

The certificate is the start, not the finish. Here's what many sellers do next.

### Open a business bank account

Take your registration documents to your bank and open an account in your business name. Customers see that name when they transfer, which builds trust. Link it to your store so payouts go there.

### Get your tax identification

Registered businesses may need a Tax Identification Number (TIN). Check current requirements with the relevant tax authority.

### Update your online presence

- **Put your business name** on your store, WhatsApp Business profile, Instagram bio and packaging.
- **Add your business name to receipts and invoices.**
- **Mention that you're registered** where it helps customers trust you, such as your store's About page.

### Keep up with annual requirements

Registered businesses may have yearly filing requirements with CAC. Check your obligations on the portal and set a reminder so your registration stays in good standing.

:::tip Set up your store with your new name
Once your business is registered, update your store name, logo and payout account at the same time so everything matches.
:::
