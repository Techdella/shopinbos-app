# App Privacy answers (App Store Connect › App Privacy)

The same data as the Google Play data safety form (`docs/play-store/data_safety.csv`), in Apple's categories. Check it
again whenever the app starts collecting something new.

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.

**Tracking:** No. The app has no advertising or tracking SDKs and doesn't link data with other companies' data for ads,
so it never shows Apple's tracking prompt.

For every type below: **Linked to the user: Yes. Used for tracking: No.**

| Contact Info | Email Address | App Functionality, Developer's Advertising or Marketing (Shopinbos emails people can turn off) |
