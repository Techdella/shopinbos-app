# The Complete Sales Page Writing Toolkit

**No copywriting experience needed.**

## How to Use This Toolkit

A sales page is the one page that tells a customer everything about your product and asks them to buy. This toolkit gives you everything you need to write your first sales page that actually sells, even if you have never written sales copy before. Work through each step in order, fill in the templates, and at the end you will have a complete sales page ready to publish on your store, link from your Instagram bio or share on WhatsApp.

**What's inside:**

- 5-step sales page writing process
- Fill-in-the-blank templates
- Headline formula library
- Power words bank
- Call-to-action (button text) examples
- Objection-handling framework
- Sales page checklist
- Self-evaluation scorecard

The best sales copy uses your customers' own words, not big English or office jargon. This research helps you understand how your ideal customers think, talk and describe their problems.

## Step 3: Write Like You Are Talking to a Friend

### Friendly Writing Checklist

Before you start writing, go through these guidelines.

**Do:**

- [ ] Use short forms (you're, don't, it's, we'll)
- [ ] Speak directly to the reader (you, your)
- [ ] Ask questions the reader will answer in their head
- [ ] Use short sentences (10 to 15 words on average)
- [ ] Keep paragraphs to 2 or 3 sentences at most, because people read on small phone screens
- [ ] Share a short personal story when it helps
- [ ] Read everything aloud to check that it flows

**Don't:**

- [ ] Use technical terms or trade jargon
- [ ] Write sentences where nobody is doing the action ("orders are processed" instead of "we pack your order")
- [ ] Write long, complicated sentences
- [ ] Use office buzzwords
- [ ] Sound too formal or stiff

### Headline Formula Library

Choose one formula and adapt it to your product.

**Formula 1: Problem to solution**
"Finally, \[get the result you want\] without \[common frustration\]"
Example: "Finally, keep your edges full without spending a fortune at the salon"

Your version: ________________________________________

**Formula 2: Transformation**
"Turn \[current state\] into \[desired state\] in \[timeframe\]"
Example: "Turn your empty wardrobe into Sunday-ready outfits in one weekend"

Your version: ________________________________________

**Formula 3: Number plus benefit**
"\[Number\] ways to \[get the benefit\] (even if \[common objection\])"
Example: "5 ways to look sharp this Detty December (even on a small budget)"

Your version: ________________________________________

**Formula 4: Question**
"What if you could \[desired outcome\] without \[pain point\]?"
Example: "What if you could serve fresh small chops at your party without cooking all night?"

Your version: ________________________________________

**Formula 5: Before and after**
"Stop \[current frustration\]. Start \[desired result\]."
Example: "Stop buying perfume oils that fade by noon. Start smelling great all day."

Your version: ________________________________________

## Step 4: Format Your Page So It Is Easy to Scan

### Formatting Checklist

Most of your customers will read on an Android phone, often with slow data. Make the page easy to skim.

**Text formatting:**

- [ ] Paragraphs of 2 or 3 sentences at most
- [ ] A subheading every 150 to 200 words
- [ ] Bullet points for lists
- [ ] Bold text for key phrases (only 1 or 2 per section)
- [ ] Enough empty space between sections
- [ ] Body text large enough to read on a phone (at least 16px)
- [ ] Strong contrast between text and background (dark text on a light background)

**Visual elements to include:**

- [ ] Main product photo at the top
- [ ] 3 to 5 clear product photos
- [ ] Lifestyle photos showing the product in use
- [ ] Customer review graphics or screenshots
- [ ] Simple icons for the benefits section
- [ ] Comparison table (if it helps)
- [ ] Short demo video (if you have one; keep the file small so it loads on mobile data)

### Benefits vs Features Table

A feature is what the product has. A benefit is what it does for the customer. Use this worksheet to turn features into benefits:

| Product feature | So what? (Benefit) | How it makes them feel |
|-----------------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Example: Double-stitched leather from Aba | Lasts for years, even with daily use | Proud and confident carrying it |
| 1. ________ | ________ | ________ |
| 2. ________ | ________ | ________ |
| 3. ________ | ________ | ________ |
| 4. ________ | ________ | ________ |
| 5. ________ | ________ | ________ |

**How to use this:**

1. List your product features in column 1
2. Ask "So what does this mean for the customer?" and fill column 2
3. Ask "How does this make them feel?" and fill column 3
4. Lead with columns 2 and 3 in your copy, then mention column 1 as proof

## Step 5: Show, Don't Just Tell

### Photo and Video Planning Worksheet

**Product photos you need:**

Main photos:

- [ ] Front view
- [ ] Side or angle view
- [ ] Back view
- [ ] Close-up of key details (stitching, texture, label)
- [ ] Size reference (next to a common object, like a phone or a bottle of water)

Lifestyle photos:

- [ ] Product in use in a real setting
- [ ] Before and after
- [ ] Product with someone who looks like your customer
- [ ] Packaging or unboxing shot
- [ ] Several products together (if you sell sets or bundles)

**Photo tips for beginners:**

- Use daylight near a window, so you do not depend on NEPA light or a ring light
- Use a simple, clean background (a plain wall or a white cloth works)
- Set your phone camera to its highest quality
- Take several angles
- Show the product in real-life use
- Show size with everyday objects

### Video Ideas

If you want to add video to your sales page, try these formats:

- [ ] 30 to 60 second product demo
- [ ] Customer review videos
- [ ] Unboxing
- [ ] Behind the scenes of how the product is made or packed
- [ ] Problem and solution explainer
- [ ] Comparison with other options
- [ ] Your story as the founder

## Call-to-Action Examples

A call to action (CTA) is the button or line that tells the customer exactly what to do next.

### Button Text Ideas

Choose or adapt one that matches your offer.

**For products:**

- "Add to Cart Now"
- "Get Yours Today"
- "Order on WhatsApp"
- "Start \[Benefit\] Now"
- "Claim Your \[Product\]"
- "Shop Now and Save"
- "Yes, I Want This"

**For services:**

- "Book Your Appointment"
- "Get Started Today"
- "Schedule Your Session"
- "Reserve Your Spot"
- "Chat With Us to Book"

**For digital products:**

- "Download Now"
- "Get Instant Access"
- "Unlock Your \[Product\]"
- "Start Learning Today"
- "Grab Your Copy"

**Custom CTA template:** "\[Action word\] + \[What they get\] + \[Time frame\]"

Examples: "Get Party-Ready Hair This Weekend", "Glow Up Your Skin Before Easter"

## Objection-Handling Framework

### Common Objections and How to Answer Them

**Objection: "It's too expensive"**

Answer by:

- [ ] Comparing it to the cost of other options
- [ ] Breaking the price down per use or per day (for example, "₦18,000 lasts 3 months, that's ₦200 a day")
- [ ] Showing what makes it special
- [ ] Offering instalments or a deposit option
- [ ] Showing how much it saves them over time

Your response:

**Objection: "I'm not sure it will work for me"**

Answer by:

- [ ] Offering a money-back or exchange guarantee
- [ ] Showing reviews from customers like them
- [ ] Giving clear details (size chart, ingredients, specifications)
- [ ] Showing how to reach you (WhatsApp number, opening hours)
- [ ] Including an FAQ section

Your response:

**Objection: "I can find it cheaper elsewhere"** (or "I fit get am for Balogun cheaper")

Answer by:

- [ ] Highlighting what only you offer
- [ ] Explaining quality differences (original vs fake, finishing, packaging)
- [ ] Showing your fast replies and reliable delivery
- [ ] Explaining your guarantee or return policy
- [ ] Showing long-term value

Your response:

**Objection: "I need to think about it"** or "I'm worried I'll pay and not receive it"

Answer by:

- [ ] Creating honest urgency (limited stock or a sale end date)
- [ ] Offering pay on delivery in your main cities, if you can
- [ ] Giving a clear return policy
- [ ] Showing real stock levels ("only 6 left")
- [ ] Making the decision easy with a guarantee

Even a short voice note or a few lines would help me a lot!

### Review Checklist

Strong reviews include:

- [ ] Specific results
- [ ] Customer's first name and city (and photo if they agree), for example "Halima, Kano"
- [ ] The problem they had before
- [ ] How your product solved it
- [ ] How they feel now
- [ ] That they would recommend you

Weak reviews to avoid:

- [ ] General praise only ("Nice product!")
- [ ] No specific details
- [ ] No name at all
- [ ] Only star ratings with no comment

## Sales Page Completion Checklist

Before you publish, make sure you have:

### Content

- [ ] A strong headline that states the main benefit
- [ ] A subheadline that expands on the promise
- [ ] An opening that speaks to the customer's problem
- [ ] 3 to 5 key benefits (not just features)
- [ ] Social proof (reviews, customer photos, number of orders delivered)
- [ ] Clear product photos
- [ ] Clear price in naira, plus delivery fees by location
- [ ] Guarantee or return policy
- [ ] FAQ section (at least 4 questions)
- [ ] Several CTA buttons through the page
- [ ] A final CTA at the bottom

### Technical

- [ ] Page works well on phones
- [ ] Loads fast, even on mobile data (under 3 seconds)
- [ ] Buy button and checkout work (place a test order)
- [ ] Secure payment through a trusted provider like Paystack or Flutterwave, with transfer, USSD or pay on delivery where possible
- [ ] Contact details visible (WhatsApp number, phone, Instagram)
- [ ] Privacy policy linked
- [ ] Padlock in the browser (the address starts with https://), which shows the page is secure

A store built on Shopinbos by Rex already handles the mobile layout, secure checkout and Nigerian payment options, so you can focus on the words.

### Getting Found and Improving

- [ ] A clear page title and short description, so the page shows well on Google
- [ ] A short description on every image (alt text), which helps Google and people with screen readers
- [ ] A simple, readable link (for example yourstore.com/ankara-two-piece)
- [ ] Visitor tracking set up (Google Analytics or your store's reports)
- [ ] A plan to test two versions of your headline or photo and keep the one that sells more (an A/B test)
