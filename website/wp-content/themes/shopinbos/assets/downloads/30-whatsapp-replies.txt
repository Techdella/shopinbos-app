30 WHATSAPP REPLIES THAT SELL
From the Shopinbos Academy · shopinbos.com/academy
Copy, paste and change the words in [brackets]. Each reply has English (EN) and Pidgin (PCM).
Save your favourites as Quick replies in WhatsApp Business: Business tools > Quick replies.

PRICE
1. EN: Hi [name]! The [product] is ₦[price]. You can order here: [store link]
   PCM: Hi [name]! The [product] na ₦[price]. You fit order here: [store link]
2. EN: It's ₦[price], and delivery to [area] is ₦[delivery]. Shall I reserve one for you?
   PCM: Na ₦[price], and delivery to [area] na ₦[delivery]. Make I keep one for you?
3. EN: That price is fixed, but if you take 2 I can do ₦[bundle price] for both.
   PCM: The price no dey move, but if you take 2 I fit do ₦[bundle price] for the two.
4. EN: All our prices are on the store, with photos and sizes: [store link]
   PCM: All our prices dey for the store, with photos and sizes: [store link]

AVAILABILITY AND SIZES
5. EN: Yes, it's available in [colours/sizes]. Which one would you like?
   PCM: Yes, e dey for [colours/sizes]. Which one you want?
6. EN: That size is sold out, but [other size] fits the same. Or I can tell you when it's back.
   PCM: That size don finish, but [other size] go fit you the same way. Or make I tell you when e land again.
7. EN: Here's a photo with the measurements so you can check the size.
   PCM: See photo with the measurement make you check the size.
8. EN: New stock arrives on [day]. Want me to save one for you?
   PCM: New stock go land on [day]. Make I keep one for you?

"IS IT ORIGINAL?"
9. EN: Yes. Here's a close-up of the [label/stitching] so you can see the quality.
   PCM: Yes o. See close-up of the [label/stitching] make you see the quality.

    PCM: Na [brand/quality] only we dey sell. If e no be wetin you see, you fit return am within [days] days.

DELIVERY
11. EN: We deliver to [areas]. Orders before [time] arrive [today/tomorrow].
    PCM: We dey deliver to [areas]. If you order before [time], e go reach you [today/tomorrow].

    PCM: Delivery to [area] na ₦[amount] and e go take [time]. You go see the rider live for WhatsApp.

    PCM: We don pack your order! See the photo. Rider go call you before e reach.
14. EN: The rider is on the way. Please keep your phone close.
    PCM: Rider don dey come. Abeg keep your phone near you.
15. EN: Sorry for the delay. The rider is [reason]. New time: [time]. Thank you for waiting.
    PCM: Sorry say e delay. The rider [reason]. New time na [time]. Thank you for waiting.

PAYMENT
16. EN: You can pay by card, transfer or USSD here: [payment link]
    PCM: You fit pay with card, transfer or USSD here: [payment link]

    PCM: You fit pay on delivery. You go get 4-digit code. Check the parcel, pay the rider, then give am the code.
18. EN: Payment received, thank you! Your order number is [number].
    PCM: We don receive your payment, thank you! Your order number na [number].
19. EN: Your order summary: [items], total ₦[total] including delivery. Reply YES to confirm.
    PCM: Your order: [items], total ₦[total] with delivery. Reply YES make we confirm.

FOLLOW-UP

AFTER DELIVERY
