## What to Sell: Product Sourcing Tools

Next, you need something to sell.

- The Tools: [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/) and verified Nigerian suppliers on Shopinbos. You can also visit markets like Balogun, Trade Fair, Alaba, Onitsha Main Market or Aba (for shoes and bags).
- Why It's Essential: Google Trends shows what people in Nigeria are searching for, so you can check that your idea has buyers. Supplier lists let you look at many products without buying stock first. With dropshipping, the supplier sends the item to your customer after you make a sale.

Your Action Step:

1. Go to Google Trends, set the country to Nigeria and type in a product idea (for example, "shea butter" or "ankara bag").
2. See if interest is growing over time or rising around seasons like Detty December or Eid.
3. Check the Shopinbos supplier marketplace or a market near you to see if the product is available at a good price.

This stops you from spending money on a product nobody wants.

## Spreading the Word: The Marketing Tools

Now, let us attract your first customers.

- The Tools: WhatsApp Business, social media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) and a free email tool ([Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) or [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/)).
- Why It's Essential: Social media is free advertising and helps you build a community. WhatsApp broadcasts and email are your direct line to people who already like what you sell.

Your Action Step:

1. Set up WhatsApp Business with your store name, opening hours and catalogue, then create a business account on one social platform.
2. Start a WhatsApp broadcast list of happy customers (only people who saved your number and agreed to updates) and create a free Mailchimp or Brevo account.
3. Add a simple "Get updates and offers" sign-up form to your store's homepage.

Start building your audience from day one.

## Understanding Your Customers: The Analytics Tool

Finally, you need to know what is working.

- The Tool: [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/), plus the insights inside Meta Business Suite.
- Why It's Essential: It tells you how people find your store and what they do there, for free. This helps you make smart choices instead of guessing.

Your Action Step:

1. Create a free Google Analytics account.
2. Connect it to your store (your store builder's help guide will show you how).
3. Check it once a week to see how many people visited and which pages they liked most.

### Your Launch Plan: A Simple Checklist

Do not get lost in the details. Follow this order:

1. Week 1: Foundation. Set up your store and add 3–5 products.
2. Week 2: Marketing. Launch your WhatsApp Business, one social media account and your sign-up form.
3. Week 3: Analytics. Connect Google Analytics.
4. Week 4: Review. Look at your numbers and social insights to see which posts and products people like.
