DELIVERY AGREEMENT
Between a shop and a dispatch rider or delivery company
From the Shopinbos Academy · shopinbos.com/academy

This is a simple template to help you agree the basics in writing. It is not legal advice.
Change the words in [brackets] and keep a signed copy each.

1. THE PEOPLE
Shop: [shop name], [address], [phone]
Rider or company: [name], [address], [phone]
Rider ID: [ID type and number]   Bike/vehicle: [plate number]
Guarantor (for individual riders): [name, phone, address]

2. WHAT THE RIDER WILL DO
- Pick up parcels from [pickup address] between [times].
- Deliver to the address on each parcel within [time] in these areas: [areas].
- Call the buyer before arriving and keep parcels clean, dry and closed.
- Never open a parcel or leave it with anyone other than the buyer or someone the buyer names.

3. PRICES
- Delivery price per parcel: [area A] ₦[amount]; [area B] ₦[amount]; [area C] ₦[amount].
- Waiting time after [minutes] minutes: ₦[amount] per [minutes] minutes.
- Failed delivery (buyer not available): ₦[amount], parcel returned the same day.
- Payment to the rider: [daily/weekly] by [transfer/cash], on [day].

4. PAY ON DELIVERY (CASH OR TRANSFER TO THE RIDER)
- The rider collects only the amount written on the parcel or in the app.
- The rider hands over the parcel only after the buyer pays and, if used, gives the delivery code.
- The rider pays all collected money to the shop by [time] the same day, to [account details].
- Any shortfall is deducted from the rider's next payment, with a written record.

5. LOST OR DAMAGED PARCELS
- The rider is responsible for each parcel from pickup until delivery.
- If a parcel is lost or damaged through the rider's fault, the rider pays [the cost price / ₦ amount], or it is claimed from the company's goods-in-transit insurance.
- Both sides take a photo of each parcel at pickup.

6. RETURNS
- If the buyer refuses a parcel, the rider brings it back to the shop by [time], unopened.

7. ENDING THIS AGREEMENT
- Either side can end this agreement with [7] days' notice in writing (WhatsApp is fine).
- All money and parcels must be settled before the last day.

8. DISAGREEMENTS
- We will first try to settle any disagreement by talking. If that fails, we will use mediation before going to court.

Signed for the shop: ____________________  Name: ____________  Date: ________
Signed by the rider/company: _____________  Name: ____________  Date: ________
