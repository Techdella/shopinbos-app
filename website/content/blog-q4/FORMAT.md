# Blog post file format

One file per post: website/content/blog-q4/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md

```
---
title: How to sell on Instagram in Nigeria: a step-by-step guide for 2026
slug: how-to-sell-on-instagram-in-nigeria
category: instagram-and-tiktok
date: 2026-10-16 08:00
cover: person-cta-left.webp
cover_bg: #E7E1FF
dek: One or two sentences, under 160 characters, shown on cards.
meta_title: Under 60 characters, keyword first
meta_description: Under 155 characters, includes the keyword, ends with a reason to click
keyword: sell on instagram in nigeria
---
```

Body: markdown. Start with 2 short intro paragraphs that answer the search question directly (no "In this article").
Use ## for main sections (6 to 10), ### for sub-sections, bullet and numbered lists, **bold** for key phrases.
Length 1,800 to 2,600 words. Use the keyword naturally in the first paragraph, one ## heading and the conclusion.
Include internal links in markdown to these site paths where relevant (2 to 5 per post):
/features/storefront/ /features/sell-on-whatsapp/ /features/social-selling/ /features/payments/ /features/pay-on-delivery/
/features/logistics/ /features/dropshipping/ /features/peggy-ai/ /marketplace/ /trending/ /pricing/ /academy/ /tools/
/resources/online-sellers-starter-kit/ /solutions/fashion/ /solutions/food/ /solutions/beauty/ /solutions/electronics/

Special blocks (each on its own lines, use 3 to 6 per post):
:::tip Title
One to three sentences.
:::
(:::warn and :::note work the same)

:::steps Title
Step heading | one-sentence description
Step heading | one-sentence description
:::

:::checklist Title
Item

:::

:::faq
Question? | Answer in one to three sentences.
:::
End every post with a :::faq block of 4 to 6 real search questions, then a final ## heading with a short next step that
