Shopinbos Business helps African businesses sell online, from one app on your phone.

Chat with Rex, our AI shop builder, and your online shop is ready in a few minutes. Take photos of what you sell and Rex writes the product names, descriptions and prices for you. Nothing goes live until you press Publish.

Speak the way you like. Shopinbos Business works in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo, and you can tap the mic to talk instead of typing.

BUILD YOUR SHOP
• Chat with Rex to set up your brand: name, colours and look
• Add products from photos, straight from your camera or gallery
• Share your shop link and QR poster on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook

SELL AND ANSWER CUSTOMERS
• One inbox for all your customer chats, with replies, notes, snooze and done
• Peggy, your AI assistant, answers customers from your own products, prices and policies
• Record sales you make in person, so all your sales are in one place
• Collect reviews and share your review link

GET PAID
• Customers pay by card or bank transfer, processed by Paystack
• Send payment links and invoices
• Money is paid straight to your bank account. Shopinbos never holds your money
• See what you've been paid in your Shopinbos Wallet

DELIVER ORDERS
• Find delivery companies near you and compare their rates
• Book riders and follow each delivery
• Mark orders as paid, sent and delivered, and call or WhatsApp the buyer

FIND PRODUCTS TO SELL
• Discover products from suppliers in Nigeria and abroad
• See the cost, your selling price and what you keep
• Dropship or buy wholesale, and ask suppliers for quotes

GROW
• Discount codes, email campaigns and marketing tips
• Peggy's daily tasks show you what to do next
• Learn as you go with short tips and videos

FOR SUPPLIERS AND DELIVERY PARTNERS
Suppliers can list products, answer quotes and fulfil orders from sellers. Delivery companies and riders can get jobs, share live location while on a delivery, and get paid.

Shopinbos Business is for small businesses, market traders, online vendors and growing brands who want to sell more without the stress.
