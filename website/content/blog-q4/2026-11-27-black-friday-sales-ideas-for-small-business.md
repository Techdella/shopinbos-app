The best **Black Friday sales ideas for small business** owners in Nigeria are simple: pick a few products you can restock, make an offer that still leaves you profit, tell your customers early, and only promise as many deliveries as you can handle. A small seller doesn't win Black Friday by cutting every price by 50%. You win by being organised while bigger shops are overloaded.

Black Friday falls on 27 November this year, and many Nigerian shoppers now expect deals across the whole week, through Cyber Monday and into December. If you're reading this on the day itself, jump to the quick wins section. If you're planning ahead for next year, start from the top.

## Why Black Friday matters for small sellers

Nigerian shoppers have grown used to Black Friday deals from big online stores. That means more people are actively looking to buy, and many are open to trying new shops if the offer and trust are right.

For small businesses, it's a chance to:

- **Win new customers** who will come back in December.
- **Clear slow-moving stock** before the festive season.
- **Raise your average order** with bundles and gifts.
- **Test offers** you might use again for Christmas and the new year.

The risk is selling more than you can pack, deliver or afford. Good planning removes most of that risk.

## Step 1: Plan your Black Friday in one page

You don't need a big strategy document. One page is enough.

:::checklist Your Black Friday plan
Dates: which days your deals run, and when they end
Products: the 5 to 15 items you will promote
Offer: what deal each product gets
Floor price: the lowest price for each item that still makes profit
Stock: how many units you have and can restock in time
Delivery: how many orders a day you and your riders can handle
Messages: what you will post and send each day
:::

### Pick your dates

Many sellers run a short window, such as Thursday to Monday, rather than a whole month. A clear end date creates urgency. "Ends Monday midnight" works better than "while stocks last" with no date.

## Step 2: Choose the right products

Not every product belongs in your Black Friday sale.

**Good choices:**
- **Best sellers** that people already ask about
- **Items with healthy margins**, so a discount still leaves profit
- **Products you can restock quickly** from your supplier
- **Gift-friendly items** for December: perfumes, bags, gadgets, beauty sets, snack hampers
- **Slow movers** you want to clear, paired with popular items

**Avoid:**
- Items where a discount would wipe out your profit
- Products you can't restock in time
- Fragile or bulky items if your delivery capacity is already tight

If you need gift-friendly stock fast, browse checked Nigerian suppliers on the [marketplace](/marketplace/) and see what's moving on [trending](/trending/). With dropshipping, the supplier ships with your shop's label, so you don't hold the stock yourself.

## Step 3: Black Friday sales ideas for small business that protect profit

Here are offers that tend to work for small businesses without turning your sale into a loss.

### 1. Bundle deals

Put two or three related items together at a slightly lower price than buying separately. A lip gloss, lip liner and mini mirror. A shirt and matching cap. A phone case, screen protector and charging cable.

Bundles raise your order value and move more stock per delivery, which also saves on delivery costs.

### 2. Free gift with purchase

Instead of discounting, add a small gift above a spend level. "Spend ₦25,000 and get a free scarf." Your cost is the gift, not a cut on everything.

### 3. Free delivery above a set amount

Delivery cost is a common reason people don't complete an order. Offer free delivery in your city on orders above a set amount. Just make sure the threshold covers the delivery cost with room for profit.

### 4. Tiered discounts

"Buy 2, get 10% off. Buy 3, get 15% off." This encourages bigger baskets and rewards customers who buy more.

### 5. Early access for loyal customers

Send your best customers the deals a day early on WhatsApp. They feel valued, and you get sales in before the rush.

### 6. Limited quantity deals

"Only 20 at this price." This is honest urgency, and it protects you from selling more than you can supply. Be truthful about the number.

### 7. Mystery boxes

Popular for fashion and beauty. Customers pay a fixed price for a box worth more. Good for clearing slow stock, as long as each box still feels like good value.

### 8. Pre-orders for December

Take Black Friday payments for items delivered in early December. You get cash now to buy stock, and customers lock in a deal.

:::tip Do the maths before you post
Use your unit cost to check every offer. If a bag costs you ₦7,200 to sell and you discount it to ₦8,000, a ₦273 payment fee and a free delivery could leave you with almost nothing.
:::

### Know your floor price

A **floor price** is the lowest price where you still make a small profit. Work it out for every Black Friday product before you set the offer.

:::warn Don't fake discounts
Raising prices the week before and then "cutting" them is easy for customers to spot, and screenshots spread fast. Real offers on real prices protect your name.
:::

## Step 4: Get your stock ready

Running out of stock halfway through Black Friday is painful. Selling items you don't have is worse.

- **Count what you have** for every product in your sale.
- **Talk to your supplier now** about how fast they can restock and in what quantities.
- **Set stock limits** in your store so items show as sold out when they run out.
- **Prepare packaging** in advance: nylons, boxes, stickers, thank-you cards.
- **Pre-pack popular bundles** so you can dispatch quickly.

If you sell on WhatsApp, Instagram and your store at the same time, keep stock in one place so you don't sell the same item twice.

## Step 5: Plan your delivery capacity

This is where many small sellers get into trouble. You get 80 orders in one day, but your rider can do 15.

### Work out your real capacity

Ask yourself:

1. How many orders can I pack in a day?
2. How many deliveries can my riders handle per day, by area?
3. How many interstate parcels can I send, and how long do they take?
4. What happens on the busiest day if a rider is sick?

### Set clear expectations

- **Tell customers the delivery timeline** at checkout and in your messages. "Black Friday orders are delivered within 3 working days in Lagos."
- **Show delivery prices at checkout** so there are no surprises.
- **Batch deliveries by area** to save time and cost.
- **Book riders early** for your busiest days.

## Step 6: Tell people early and often

Your customers can't buy a deal they don't know about.

:::steps Black Friday promotion schedule
One week before | Post a teaser on Status, Instagram and TikTok saying deals are coming and when.
Three days before | Share a sneak peek of two or three offers and ask people to save your number.
The day before | Send early access to loyal customers on WhatsApp with the product links.
Black Friday morning | Post every deal with prices, photos and a link to order and pay.
During the weekend | Post updates: what's selling out, what's left, time remaining.
Last day | Send a final reminder with the end time.
:::

## Quick wins and what to do after

### If Black Friday is today

If you're reading this on the day, you can still take part:

1. **Pick three products** with good margins and stock on hand.
2. **Choose one simple offer**, like a bundle or free delivery above a set amount.
3. **Post on Status and Instagram** now with prices and your store link.
4. **Message your last 30 customers** personally on WhatsApp.
5. **Set an end time**, such as Monday midnight, and stick to it.

### After Black Friday

The sale ends, but the work isn't done.

- **Deliver on time** and message customers after delivery.
- **Ask for a review or photo** from happy customers.
- **Note what sold** and what didn't, for your December plan.
- **Save new customers' details** so you can share Christmas offers.
- **Check your profit**, not just your sales total.

## Frequently asked questions

:::faq
What are good Black Friday sales ideas for small business? | Bundles, a free gift above a spend level, free delivery over a set amount, tiered discounts, early access for loyal customers and limited-quantity deals all work well without wiping out profit.
When is Black Friday in Nigeria? | Black Friday is the Friday after the American Thanksgiving, which is 27 November in 2026. Many Nigerian shops run deals across the whole week and into Cyber Monday.
How much discount should I give on Black Friday? | Only as much as your margin allows. Work out your floor price for each product first, and consider gifts or bundles instead of deep cuts.
How do I handle extra delivery orders during Black Friday? | Know how many orders you can pack and deliver per day, book riders early, batch by area and tell customers the delivery timeline upfront.
Should small businesses do Black Friday at all? | If you plan stock, offers and delivery, it's a good way to win new customers before December. If you can't fulfil orders well, a smaller offer is better than none.
:::
