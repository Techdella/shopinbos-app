Is the Starter plan really free?

Yes. There’s no monthly fee. You only pay a small fee when a customer pays you.

When do I get paid?

Payments are paid out to your bank account through Paystack, usually within a few working days.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade any time from the app.

What are Rex and Peggy credits?

Credits cover building, writing and drafting. Every plan includes some each month, and you can top up. [Confirm amounts.]

Do you charge for delivery?

Delivery prices are set by the rider or company you book, and you see the price before you book.
